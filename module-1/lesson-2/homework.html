Tip of the week

The “When exactly?” test

Portuguese often uses one past tense where English needs two. Before you choose, ask yourself one question: can I answer “When exactly?” with a finished time?

Yes → past simple“I moved to Lisbon in 2019.”
“She failed the exam in her first year.”
No, or it's not important → present perfect“I've lived in three countries.”
“To date, I've published two papers.”

Practise it this week: when you read an article in English, find one sentence with each tense and ask “When exactly?”
