Tip of the week

Silence is a signpost

When Brazilian speakers need time to think in English, they often fill the gap with Portuguese sounds: é…, tipo…, né? Your listener notices them immediately, and they can make you come across as less confident. In English, a short pause sounds more confident than a filler. When you really need time, use an English phrase instead:

✗ é… né? tipo…✓ (a short pause)
✓ Well, …✓ Let me think about that for a second.
✓ That's a good question.

Try this: record yourself answering one question for 30 seconds. Count the fillers. Record again and try to replace every filler with a pause.
