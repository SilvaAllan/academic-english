Who Are You, Really?

New Students Forum · posted by Tomás

Hi everyone! I'm Tomás, from Buenos Aires. I've just started a master's in marine biology and I don't know anyone yet. My research focuses on coral reefs and how they adapt to warmer oceans. In my free time I surf (badly) and play the drums. My flatmate convinced me to write this post, so here I am. Does anyone want to grab a coffee this week? Ask me anything!

Tip of the week

The A-A-A rule for conversations that don't die

Short answers end conversations. When someone asks you a question, do three things:

Answer“I'm from São Bernardo.”
Add“It's near São Paulo, famous for cars.”
Ask back“What about you? Where did you grow up?”

Practise it this week: every time you answer a question in English, add one detail and ask one back.
